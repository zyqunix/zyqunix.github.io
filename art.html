Music

My music is mostly ambient or lowercase. Both of these are a minimalist genre meant to be played as background music or as a background noise. The first real ambient music I've really enjoyed were D Love Arsenal's works in walkedoutneimans' games. As well as Josephino Moriko, Ana Jikia, Glint, Lilic, Rogergoon, Tek Lintowe and Nobo໓𝓎 ⠀⠀⠀impo૨ta♫t . All of them are really good artists and I really recommend giving all of them a listen if you like this type of music.
Inspiration beside, I compose my music in FL Studio 25 and Tenacity/Audacity. Every song is composed with stock, built-in plugins and presets, (field) recordings. I am by no means a professional composer, though it's fun creating something I like and something that brings me joy. I also really love seeing people be happy and telling me my music is a good background noise.
My earlier tracks were mostly experimenting with the plugins and to help me learn the software.

I cannot show them here due to limitations - please visit my SoundCloud instead.

Art

As you've read on the home screen, I make my art in GIMP and Snapseed. It started off as an Inspiration and take on walkedoutneimans' posts and art style - which i've seen in his games iPad Baby, Tyko's Dying Together and Suburban Basketball. Ville Kallio's art influenced me in that way as well, with his game Cruelty Squad, which made me want to make a game like his; though I've never finished it because of school and a lack of motivation... Their art made me realise that photography and editing isn't just basic colour grading and cropping; it made me realise that art can be anything you put your mind to and what you desire. If you think that the colours should be inverted and it looks good in your eyes, so be it; no one should be able to stop people from creating and publishing art. Art is what made the world, the world; our society, the society we have today. Keep going don't stop.
