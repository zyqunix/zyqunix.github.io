Welcome to my site!

I am a year old Artist, Musician and Programmer! I usually spend my time making new art, which can be found on my Instagram. I really enjoy early 2010's websites, clothing, memes and games.

I make art for people who like abstract forms of art, or people who like thinking about the meaning behind the piece. I write 2000 character essays about my point of view and how I view it, but only after it's finished and while it's being uploaded so I can look back and truly think about the art; there are many truthful and intelligent statements - however some aren't subjective. My art is mainly made in Snapseed and the GNU Image Manipulator. My first pieces were inspired by walkedoutneimans and Mr. Yuk but then I changed it to my own art style later.

The music I create is made in FL Studio 25 with its stock & built-in plugins. The genre I compose is ambient, some could argue it's lowercase. I was mostly inspired by D Love Arsenal (and his alts: Michael Series and Crystal Coral), Josephine Moriko and limly.

Visuals I make are mostly in video form and are edits. I use Vegas Pro 22, ffmpeg, Avidemux and have used kdenlive in the past. The posts can be found on my TikTok.
